FUTURE WORK CAFÉ 2035

Future Work Café 2035 explores a hybrid café and flexible workspace organized around social activity, collaboration, focused work, and biophilic design. The selected Biophilic Work Islands concept uses planting as a functional spatial organizer while creating a gradual transition from active social areas to quieter work spaces.

Concept Visualization

The concept establishes a warm biophilic environment combining café, social, collaborative, and focused work activities. Planting, natural materials, open circulation, and daylight help define the spatial character.

Spatial Organization + Circulation

The floor plan organizes the space along a clear active-to-quiet sequence: Entrance → Café + Social → Collaborative Work → Quiet Work. A primary circulation path connects the different zones, while planter boundaries create separation without completely isolating them.

Blender Spatial Development

The first Blender version was used to evaluate zoning, circulation, major furniture placement, and basic spatial proportions before developing materials and details.

Final Blender View

The refined eye-level view shows the primary circulation path connecting the café/social, collaborative, and quiet work zones. Planting creates soft spatial boundaries while maintaining visual openness across the space.

Design Conclusion

The final design develops the café as a connected sequence of social, collaborative, and focused work environments. Planting works as both a spatial and visual boundary, while the primary circulation path maintains clear movement and visual connection across the space.
