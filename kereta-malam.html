KERETA MALAM

Elvy Sukaesih · Indonesian Dangdut

Kereta Malam is an album-cover reinterpretation inspired by Elvy Sukaesih’s Indonesian dangdut song. The design explores an overnight train journey through the concept ‘Moonlight to Morning,’ translating movement, changing time, landscape, and the experience of travel into a visual narrative.

Final Album Cover

Color Direction

The palette moves from midnight indigo and moonlit blue toward dawn amber and warm cream, supporting the visual transition from night to morning.

Initial Experiment

The first experiment established the passenger-eye train-window perspective, railway movement, and the transition between nighttime and morning. The result created a strong sense of travel, but the change from moonlight to sunrise was too dramatic.

Final Refinement

The final refinement preserved the established visual concept while improving typography, margins, alignment, and overall graphic balance. The title gained more breathing room while the artist name and year remained secondary elements.

Design Conclusion

The final design combines the ‘Moonlight to Morning’ narrative with the ‘Temporal Train Window’ visual direction. The moving foreground, Indonesian landscape, passenger reflection, fading moonlight, and emerging morning light communicate an overnight journey through Java while maintaining a clear album-cover identity.
